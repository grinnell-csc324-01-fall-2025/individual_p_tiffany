# Evaluation Summary: AI Therapy Website

## Evaluators
- Shaocai Hong  
- Aven Tang  

## Key Strengths Identified

### Visual Design & Emotional Tone
- The background color was described as calming and emotionally soothing, contributing positively to the therapeutic atmosphere of the website.
- The logo design was highly appreciated for its simplicity and clarity. Evaluators noted that it effectively communicates the intended relationship between AI and love, aligning well with the project’s core concept.
- The slogan was considered eye-catching and successful in drawing user attention.

### AI Responses & Therapeutic Tools
- The AI-generated responses were perceived as helpful and constructive, offering practical suggestions to users.
- The breathing exercises included in the toolkit were specifically highlighted as useful and effective for anxiety relief.
- Overall, the website was described as human-centered and thoughtfully designed from a user perspective.


## Areas for Improvement

### Interaction & Usability
- On the chat interface, messages can only be sent by clicking the “Send” button. Evaluators suggested enabling the Enter key for message submission to improve usability and interaction flow.
- Currently, conversations can only be conducted via text. The absence of **voice messaging** was noted as a limitation, as voice-based interaction could enhance emotional expression and accessibility.

### Conversational Experience
- While the AI’s responses are helpful, their consistent use of structured, bullet-point advice made the interaction feel more like studying than conversing.
- It was suggested that bullet-pointed advice be interwoven with more natural, human-like conversational responses to create a warmer and more engaging dialogue experience.

### Feature Expansion & Differentiation
- The platform was noted to feel somewhat similar to ChatGPT in its current form. To better highlight the uniqueness of the AI therapy experience, evaluators recommended:
  - Adding guided meditation features for anxiety relief.
  - Allowing users to select meditation music.
  - Enabling the AI to generate customized, AI-voiced meditation audio, which would better demonstrate the strengths of AI personalization.

### Navigation & Organization
- The menu bar font was considered too small, and the menu itself could not be collapsed, which caused it to take up unnecessary screen space.
- There is currently no way to categorize or organize conversations, making it harder to manage or revisit past discussions as the number of conversations grows.

### Visual Polish
- While the overall design was seen as user-friendly, evaluators felt that adding more subtle animations or transitions could make the interface feel more polished and modern. At present, the site contains very limited animation effects.
